# Bluesky launch skeets

Twenty posts announcing the launch of Thing Factory (https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com). Each is under Bluesky's 300-character limit.

## 1

Thing Factory is live! 🎉 Build a contraption on a hex board, one Thing at a time, while it runs. Crates come out of a source; get them to the target. Every Thing does exactly one thing, every tick.

Free, in your browser: https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 2

A Belt moves a crate one hex. A Spring flings it three. A Rocket blasts it up to eight. A Puller reaches across and yanks. Each Thing does one thing, and the fun is what happens when they overlap.

https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 3

Every command ticks the world once. Place a Thing, tick. Discard, tick. Wait, tick. Building happens while the factory runs, so every placement is a bet on where the crates will be.

https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 4

There are gremlins. They wander. They ride your belts. Sometimes a gremlin rides a belt straight into the sea, and you laugh, and a new one climbs out on the shore.

Thing Factory: https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 5

No timer. No game over. No defeat. Milestones at 10, 20, 50, 100, 200, 500, 1000 delivered crates, and on. Your score is the turn you reached each one. The factory never ends.

https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 6

Each milestone unlocks a new card. You start with Belts, Bridges, Walls and Traps. Then Pushers and Pullers, the Slide, the Spring, the Mover, the Cannon, the Rocket, and the Bomb last. One new toy each time the factory pays off.

https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 7

Ice makes things slide. Water sinks them. Mountains block. A Spring hops a lake. A crate skids across ice into another crate and shoves the whole line two hexes on. Nobody scripted that; it's one shared movement rule.

https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 8

Thing Factory animates every tick hop by hop, in lockstep, so when your plan breaks you can point at the exact hex where it went wrong. Then you fix it. Then a gremlin finds a new way to break it.

https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 9

Pick your board: 16×10 fits the screen. 20×20, 30×30 and 60×60 pan. On the bigger boards each milestone opens a new crate color with its own source and target, up to four, so lines cross and have to split.

https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 10

Pushers and Pullers only work on one crate color (and every gremlin). That's how you split a mixed line: red goes left, blue goes on. Pick the color when you place it.

Thing Factory: https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 11

Two Springs throwing into the same hex blow both crates up. I didn't design that joke. The rules did. 💥

https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 12

Now and then a crate comes out big: drawn bigger, worth 10 delivered, or 10 lost. Rare early, commoner with every milestone. Guard them.

Thing Factory: https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 13

The Cannon lobs a crate clean over the next four hexes: water, mountains, walls, gremlins, it touches none of them. Then it lands in the fifth like it was on a Belt. Very satisfying.

https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 14

Gremlins pounce. Any crate next to a gremlin is a crate at risk, even one riding a belt. That's what Walls and Traps beside a line are for. A Trap catches them; crates walk over it safe.

https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 15

Thing Factory has goals: three at a time, each a step on one of the game's counters. Clear one and another takes its place. Your lifetime totals become achievements.

https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com

## 16

Sign in with Bluesky (optional) and your achievements follow you from computer to computer. No account needed to play.

Thing Factory: https://ttdat.yuwakisa.com
